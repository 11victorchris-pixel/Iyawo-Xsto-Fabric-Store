OUR BRAND STORY

Founded in Ilorin,
Worn Across Nigeria.

IYAWO XSTO Fabric Store began in Ilorin, Kwara State, with a simple belief: everyone deserves beautiful, high-quality fabric at a fair price — whether buying one yard for personal use or hundreds of yards for resale and Aso Ebi.

What started as a single table at Ago Market has grown into two full retail outlets — at Ago Market and Zambari Market — plus a fast-growing online store serving wholesale and retail customers nationwide. We stock Swiss lace, Organza, Coupon lace, Beaded lace, Aso Ebi, Senator materials, Dry lace, Ankara, Damask and many more curated collections.
